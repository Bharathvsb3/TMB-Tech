GasOne ("the App", "we", "us") is a collection-management app used by gas agencies to record daily cylinder deliveries, cash collection, and stock. GasOne is developed and operated by TMB Tech. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Who this applies to

GasOne is provided to gas agencies and used by their staff (Admins, Users, Delivery Boys, and Store Keepers) under an account created by their agency's Admin. There is no public self-signup. Each agency's data is kept separate from every other agency using the App.

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Mobile number & passwordUsed to sign in to your account. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form; nobody at GasOne, including Admins, can view your actual password.
Username & roleIdentifies you within your agency and determines which screens you can access.
Transaction & collection recordsCylinder counts, cash denominations, dates, and related notes you or your agency enter. This is the core data the App exists to record.
Profile photo (optional)If you choose to upload one, it's shown alongside your account within your own agency.
Camera accessUsed only if you choose to take a new photo for your profile picture. The App does not access the camera for any other purpose.
Biometric data (fingerprint/face)If you turn on biometric login, your device's own operating system handles the fingerprint/face match. GasOne never receives or stores your biometric data; it only stores a device-local flag saying biometric login is enabled.

How we use this information

  • To run day-to-day collection and stock-recording operations for your agency.
  • To let your agency's Admin review, approve, and report on collection activity.
  • To keep your account secure (sign-in, session handling).

Data sharing

Your data is never sold, and never shared with any third party outside your own agency. Data is only visible to:

  • You, for your own entries.
  • Your agency's Admin and, where applicable, Users with the relevant permission.
  • GasOne's own technical operators at TMB Tech, solely to operate, host, and maintain the App, never for marketing or resale.

Data storage & retention

Data is stored on servers used to operate the App and backed up periodically for recovery purposes. Transaction and account records are retained for as long as your agency's account remains active, plus a reasonable backup-retention period afterward, in line with your agency's own record-keeping needs.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, contact your agency's Admin, or reach us directly using the details below. We will coordinate with your agency to action the request.

Requesting data deletion

Email us at the address below with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including your registered mobile number and agency name. We will confirm and process the request, noting that some transaction records may need to be retained where your agency has a legal or accounting obligation to keep them.

Children's privacy

GasOne is a business tool for gas agency staff and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
