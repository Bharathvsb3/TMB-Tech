What GasOne does

The day's cash, cylinders and stock register in one place

Built around how an LPG gas agency actually closes its day: delivery boys come back with cash, the office counts it, the Admin checks it, and the store keeper fills the register.

Denomination entry

One row per delivery boy: cylinders delivered for each product, then the notes and coins from ₹500 down to ₹1. The grand total adds itself up.

Gas Value match

The cash is checked against the cylinders' gas value. It turns green when they match, and red with the exact difference when they don't.

Admin approval

Every entry starts as Pending. The Admin approves it, or rejects it with a reason. A delivery boy's day locks once saved, unless the Admin allows a re-edit.

DGS cylinder register

The store keeper fills the day's register: filled and empty cylinders for 10Kg composite, 14.2Kg domestic and 19Kg commercial, with invoice numbers. It locks after saving.

Reports & exports

Filter by date range, delivery boy, who entered it and status. Export to PDF or Excel, share either one, or print straight away.

Expenses & Net Total

The Admin records the day's expenses. The dashboard shows today's collection, gas value, cylinder count, expense and the net total after expenses.

How a day runs

From the delivery round to a closed day

Four steps, each done by the person who should be doing it.

Cash and cylinders are entered

Each delivery boy's cylinders and notes go into the denomination grid, by them or by the office.

GasOne checks the match

The grand total is compared with the gas value. Any difference shows in red, down to the rupee.

The Admin approves

Pending entries are approved or rejected with a reason. Expenses are added to get the net total.

The register is filed

The store keeper submits the DGS cylinder register, and reports are ready to export or print.

Questions

Good to know

Does GasOne work without internet?

GasOne needs an internet connection to save entries. If the connection drops while you're filling the grid, the unsaved rows stay on the device so you can save them once you're back online.

Can a delivery boy see other delivery boys' entries?

No. A Delivery Boy account opens only their own denomination entry. The store keeper sees only the day's register.

Is our agency's data shared with anyone?

No. It is never sold and never shared outside your agency. Each agency's data is kept separate. See the privacy policy.

Which languages does it support?

English and Tamil. Each person can switch at any time.

Where does it run?

On Android phones, and in a web browser on the office computer, where it gets a full sidebar layout.
