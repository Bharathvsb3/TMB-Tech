Delete your SharkPanda account data

Delete your account and its data

SharkPanda works without an account. An account exists only if you chose Sign in with Google to join TMB Coins or Refer & Earn. Deleting it removes what TMB Tech holds about you on its servers.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SharkPanda and go to Settings → More.
  2. Open TMB Coins (or tap the coin balance in Steps), then Account.
  3. Tap Remove my email & data (also shown as Delete my account data) and confirm. You need an internet connection.

By e-mail (if you cannot open the app)

Write to info@tmbtech.in from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my SharkPanda data. We delete your data and reply within 30 days.

Delete only some of your data (keep the app)

You can delete data without deleting anything else you use in the app:

  • TMB Coins data: the same Remove my email & data button above.
  • Usage statistics: open Settings and turn off Share anonymous usage statistics. New events stop at once.
  • Browsing history, bookmarks, downloads: stored only on your phone. Clear them in the app, or uninstall.
  • HeartConnect: chats, notes, memories and photos live on your phone. HeartConnect → Remove partner deletes them all.
  • Your device record: write to info@tmbtech.in and we delete the record of your installation.

Anything stored only on your phone (history, bookmarks, HeartConnect) is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall SharkPanda.
